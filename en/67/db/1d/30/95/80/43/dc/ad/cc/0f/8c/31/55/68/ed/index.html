What Is Codeforces?

By @null_djc · Series

If you've ever been drawn to solving problems with code, you've surely heard of Codeforces: a competitive programming platform where thousands of people spend hours thinking about how to solve exercises, more for the love of the challenge than anything else. In this series we'll solve problems little by little, showing how we think through each solution, where we get stuck, and how we reach the final answer, all explained simply and assuming nothing.

What you need to follow along

Just three things: a free account on codeforces.com, a language you feel comfortable in (C++ and Python are the classics, but it doesn't matter which you use) and the willingness to make mistakes in public, because we do too.

The problems we solve/analyze will keep appearing here, one by one, with an explanation of how we approached it, what the test cases check and what the verdict taught us.
